A Little Age is a cozy minimalist city builder and strategy simulation developed and published by Benerot. It released on August 28, 2026, for Windows PC. Players expand a civilization from a small hamlet into a connected network of settlements, each with its own local economy.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **A Little Age**. This trainer enhances A Little Age's settlement-building and resource-management systems. It provides control over Food, Materials, Gold, delivery operations, construction progress, map visibility, game speed, and advancement progression while preserving the game's relaxed single-player format.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with settlement layouts, test regional economies, complete matches more quickly, explore procedural maps freely, or reduce resource-management constraints in the single-player campaign.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Food, Materials, and Gold** — Prevents the selected settlement's core resources from being depleted during construction, production, and regional support.
- **Freeze Production** — Stops settlement production and resource consumption while keeping the game available for planning and inspection.
- **Instant Construction** — Finishes queued buildings and settlement improvements without waiting for their normal construction duration.
- **Pause Without Limits** — Keeps the simulation paused while allowing construction planning, settlement management, and other supported actions.
- **Game Speed Control** — Adjusts the simulation speed to make production, deliveries, and settlement growth faster or slower.

### 💰 Economy

- **Instant Resource Delivery** — Completes active delivery jobs immediately, allowing resources to move between settlements without normal travel time.
- **Settlement Stability** — Protects supported settlements from resource shortages and helps maintain their local economies.

### 🌍 World

- **Reveal Map** — Reveals procedural terrain, biomes, settlements, and resource locations across the current match.

### 📌 Progression

- **Unlock Advancements** — Unlocks available civilization advancements without requiring the usual progression steps.
- **Complete Great Temple Progress** — Completes or accelerates progress toward the Great Temple objective in the current civilization.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

A Little Age is managed from a top-down strategic view rather than through direct character movement. Use the camera to inspect settlements and plan regional connections. The trainer's map reveal option helps locate unexplored terrain, biomes, resources, and settlement sites.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional character inventory. Its economy is built around Food, Materials, and Gold stored and produced by individual settlements. Select a settlement before activating resource options so the trainer applies them to the intended local economy.

### World & Environment

Each match generates a unique procedural world with different biomes, resources, and settlement conditions. Reveal the map to study the whole region, then use delivery and stability options to connect settlements whose local economies cannot meet their own needs.

### Combat & Weapons

A Little Age is a peaceful settlement-building and resource-management game without character combat, armies, or enemy encounters. Trainer options focus on construction, trade, production, exploration, and civilization advancement instead of weapons or combat statistics.
